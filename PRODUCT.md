# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, bioinformaticians, clinicians and data scientists, mostly in Thailand and the wider region, deciding whether to apply for one of about 50 seats at GeTH Hackathon 2027 (7–12 February 2027, Chiang Mai). They arrive from an email, a colleague or social media, usually on a laptop at work or a phone between tasks. In one visit they need to understand what the event is, what data they will work with, whether they qualify, when the deadlines are, and how to apply. Secondary readers are funders and partners (HSRI, CMU Faculty of Medicine, NSTDA, Genomics Thailand) checking that the event is presented credibly.

## Product Purpose

A static public site that promotes the Genomics Thailand (GeTH) Hackathon and turns qualified visitors into applicants through a Google Form. It explains the event's clinical and biological goals, the 50,000-genome dataset (aggregate counts only; analysis happens in NSTDA's Trusted Research Environment), dates and venue, schedule, selection criteria, terms and code of conduct, and organisers. Success means a visitor understands the event, trusts it with sensitive national data, and applies before the December 2026 deadline.

## Brand Personality

Precise, bold, open. Swiss-style rigour and confident scale (inspired by the "silence." reference site and the spirit of DBCLS BioHackathon), welcoming to every discipline in the room. Visitors should leave with scientific credibility (a serious national programme that handles sensitive data properly), excitement to hack (hands-on, collaborative, "I want to be in Chiang Mai"), and clarity (what, when, who and how, without hunting). Copy is plain and specific; it names real diseases, data types and dates rather than abstractions.

## Anti-references

- Startup SaaS landing pages: the hero-metric template, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids, gradient text and buzzword copy (empower, unlock, seamless, next-generation).

## Design Principles

1. **Show the real data, never decoration.** Imagery comes from the dataset itself (the genome barcode, the dataset map) and stays aggregate-only.
2. **Credibility through restraint.** Strict grid, one accent, plain statements about governance (TRE, PDPA, NDA and data-use agreements); nothing that reads as hype.
3. **Every section answers an applicant's question.** What is it, what data, when and where, what happens each day, can I apply, who runs it.
4. **Bold where it earns attention, quiet everywhere else.** Scale and the accent colour go to the event name, the key numbers and the Apply action.
5. **Open to every discipline.** Clinicians and data scientists should both feel addressed; avoid jargon walls and insider shorthand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. AA contrast in both light (default) and dark themes, visible keyboard focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (the marquee stops and can be paused on hover or focus), text alternatives for the barcode and dataset map, and no information carried by colour alone. English only.
